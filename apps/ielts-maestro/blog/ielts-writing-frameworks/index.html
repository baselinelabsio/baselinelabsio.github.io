Structuring High-Scoring Essays: Proven Frameworks for Writing Tasks 1 and 2

IELTS Maestro Team·Oct 23, 2026·6 min read

Staring at a blank page and watching the clock tick down? Master the architectural blueprints that guarantee top-band essays.

In this post

  1. The anatomy of Task 1: data precision & overview
  2. The architecture of Task 2: balanced argumentation
  3. FAQ

The anatomy of Task 1: data precision & overview

Whether you face a line graph, process diagram, or table, Task 1 requires an objective, structured breakdown rather than a subjective opinion.

  • The introduction: Paraphrase the prompt using synonyms to state clearly what the visual data illustrates.
  • The overview: This is the most crucial paragraph. Summarize the major trends, overall trajectories, or primary stages without listing raw numbers.
  • Details paragraphs: Group data logically — such as comparing highest-performing categories in one paragraph and contrasting lower tiers in the next — citing specific data points as evidence.

The architecture of Task 2: balanced argumentation

Task 2 demands a persuasive, well-defended essay layout that directly answers the specific prompt type (e.g., opinion, discussion, or problem-solution).

  • Four-paragraph framework: Stick to a reliable structure consisting of an introduction (hook, background, and clear thesis statement), two distinct body paragraphs featuring a topic sentence, explanation, and concrete example, and a concise conclusion that restates your position.
  • Cohesive progression: Ensure each paragraph transitions naturally into the next using lexical links rather than mechanical, overused transitional phrases.

Frequently asked questions

How much time should I spend on Task 1 vs Task 2?

The recommended split is 20 minutes for Task 1 and 40 minutes for Task 2 — Task 2 is worth twice as much in your overall writing band.

Is the overview paragraph in Task 1 really that important?

Yes — missing a clear overview will cap your Task Achievement score at Band 5, regardless of how detailed the rest of your response is.

How many words should each essay be?

Task 1: minimum 150 words. Task 2: minimum 250 words. Underwriting the limit automatically reduces your Task Achievement score.
