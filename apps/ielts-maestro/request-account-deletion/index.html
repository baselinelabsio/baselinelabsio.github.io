Request account deletion

This is the official IELTS Maestro account deletion request page. It is intended for users who want us to remove their IELTS Maestro account and associated personal data. For general privacy details, see the IELTS Maestro Privacy Policy.

What will be deleted

  • Your IELTS Maestro account record.
  • Personal profile data, including name and email.
  • Any saved test progress, speaking/writing submissions, and recommendations tied to your account.

What may be retained

  • Aggregated anonymous analytics used for app improvement.
  • Legal or security records if required by law.

Retention periods may vary depending on legal and security obligations. We will notify you if additional verification is required.

Prefer email? Send your request to support@prepmaestro.com and include the name and email tied to your IELTS Maestro account.
