Logo Lab — monospace wordmark concepts

Eight directions built around a developer/coding typeface. Because the exact font wasn't named, the concepts are spread across seven different well-known coding fonts — each one is labelled, so you can point at the one you were thinking of. Every concept is shown on white and on dark, plus at roughly 32px to prove it survives favicon and nav sizes. Just tell us a number.
