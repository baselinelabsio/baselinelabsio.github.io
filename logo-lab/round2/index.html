Blending Option 3 & Option 5

Six blends of the two you picked: the terminal prompt with a blinking cursor and the snake_case variable name with an accented underscore. Each one varies a different axis — prompt or no prompt, block cursor or underscore cursor or none, and which element carries the accent. Four in the brand blues, two in ember. Every option includes a typing preview at the bottom, since that is how it will behave on the landing page.
